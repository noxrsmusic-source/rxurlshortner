Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

How is 99% profit share different from a fixed CPM?

Instead of locking you into a "guaranteed" low rate, we share 99% of the actual ad revenue your traffic generates. When advertiser rates are high, your earnings go up automatically — no ceiling.

How does the 10% lifetime referral commission work?

Every user gets a unique referral link in their dashboard. When someone signs up through your link, you permanently earn 10% of everything they make on RX URL Shorter — for life. There is no cap, no expiry, and the commission is automatically added to your account.

Why are the rates "estimated" and not fixed?

Because we pay you 99% of real ad revenue (not a fixed CPM), your actual earnings depend on live advertiser demand, traffic quality and season. The tier rates are a baseline — you can earn more.

My country isn't in the list. Do I still earn?

Yes. Traffic from any country not listed in our Tier 1–3 table still earns the $1.00 per 1,000 clicks default rate — and you still keep 99% of the ad revenue generated.

When and how do I get paid?

Payouts are processed on-demand once you reach the $5 minimum. We currently support PayPal, USDT, and direct bank transfer, with more options rolling out.

Are there any traffic limits or hidden fees?

None. No hidden fees, no forced traffic caps, no exclusivity requirements. You can use RX URL Shorter alongside other platforms.
