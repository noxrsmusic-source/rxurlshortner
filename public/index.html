Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

How is 99% profit share different from a fixed CPM?

Instead of a low guaranteed rate, we share a large percentage of actual ad revenue. When advertiser rates rise, your earnings rise automatically.

How does the lifetime referral commission work?

Every user gets a unique referral link. When someone signs up through it, you permanently earn a percentage of their earnings — for life, no cap.

Why are the rates "estimated" and not fixed?

Because actual earnings depend on live advertiser demand, traffic quality, and season. The tier rates are a baseline.

My country isn't in the list. Do I still earn?

Yes. Traffic from any country not listed earns the default rate.

When and how do I get paid?

Payouts are processed on-demand once you reach the minimum. We support PayPal, USDT, UPI, and bank transfer.

Are there any traffic limits or hidden fees?

None. No hidden fees, no forced traffic caps.
